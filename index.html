Garlic Butter Pasta — Import Test

This is a temporary test recipe, not one of your saved family recipes. We're testing FamilyWall's website importer before publishing your real recipes.

Prep: 5 minutes · Cook: 15 minutes · Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 4 ounces dry spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water according to package instructions; drain, reserving a little pasta water.
  2. Melt butter over low heat, add minced garlic, and cook for about 1 minute without browning.
  3. Toss pasta with garlic butter, salt, and a splash of pasta water if needed.
  4. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve.
