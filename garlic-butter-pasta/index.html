Garlic Butter Pasta — Import Test

Simple garlic butter spaghetti to test importing a custom recipe into FamilyWall.

Prep: 5 minutes · Cook: 15 minutes · Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 4 ounces dry spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water according to package directions, then drain, reserving a little pasta water.
  2. Melt butter over low heat, add garlic and cook for about one minute without browning.
  3. Toss pasta with garlic butter and salt, adding a splash of pasta water if needed.
  4. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve.
